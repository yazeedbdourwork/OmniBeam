AirDrop for Windows and Android: the browser alternative

AirDrop only works between Apple devices. OmniBeam gives you the same tap-and-send idea between any devices, because it runs in the web browser.

What you get

  • Devices on the same Wi‑Fi find each other automatically, like AirDrop.
  • Works between iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Android, Linux and Chromebook.
  • Files go directly from device to device and are encrypted end to end.
  • No size limit for direct transfers, no account and nothing to install.
  • Devices on different networks can pair once with a 6‑digit code.

AirDrop compared with OmniBeam

  • Devices: AirDrop is Apple only; OmniBeam works on any device with a modern browser.
  • Distance: AirDrop needs the devices nearby; OmniBeam also works across the internet.
  • Sharing with others: OmniBeam can also make an encrypted link that anyone can open later.

More details in OmniBeam vs AirDrop.

Make it feel like an app

Install OmniBeam from the browser (Install in Chrome or Edge, Add to Home Screen on iPhone). On Windows, get the free OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store; see OmniBeam for Windows.

Unlike AirDrop, OmniBeam needs no Bluetooth and no Apple account. Both devices only need a browser, and every transfer is encrypted end to end, so the files stay private even when they go through the relay server.

OmniBeam is a free alternative to AirDrop for Windows and Android that runs in the browser.

Questions and answers

Is there an official AirDrop for Windows or Android?

No. AirDrop is built into Apple devices only, and Apple has not released it for Windows or Android. To get the same quick sharing between an iPhone and a PC or an Android phone, you need a tool that works on both sides, such as OmniBeam in the browser.

How close is OmniBeam to AirDrop?

Very close in daily use: devices on the same Wi‑Fi appear automatically, you tap one and choose the files, and the other device accepts them. The differences are that OmniBeam runs in a browser tab instead of the system menu, and that it also works across different networks after pairing once.

Can I send to a Windows PC that has no browser open?

The page has to be open on the receiving device, just as AirDrop needs the receiver to be awake and visible. On Windows, the free OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store makes this easier: it starts with the PC, so files can arrive without opening a browser first.

Does it work with iPhone photos?

Yes. Photos and videos arrive in full quality, and iPhone HEIC photos can be saved as JPG so they open on any Windows PC or Android phone.

Is it safe?

Every transfer is encrypted end to end on your device, and nothing is stored on a server on the way.

Can several devices receive at once?

Yes. Use “Send to everyone” to send the same files to every device that is open, or join a public room so a group of phones and computers can share with each other. This is handy in a classroom or a meeting, where AirDrop would only reach the Apple devices in the room.

Does it work offline?

The devices need an internet connection to find each other, even though the files themselves travel directly over your Wi‑Fi whenever possible.
