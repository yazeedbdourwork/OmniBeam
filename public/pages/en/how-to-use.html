How to use OmniBeam, step by step

OmniBeam sends files and text between your devices straight from the browser. Here is everything it can do, step by step.

1. Devices on the same Wi‑Fi

  1. Open omnibeam.site on both devices.
  2. Each device shows the other one automatically, with a random name like “Blue Fox”. Tap your name at the bottom to rename this device.
  3. Tap the other device and choose files, or drag and drop files onto it. On a computer you can also hover over it and pick Send files or Send message.
  4. The other device asks to accept. After that the files arrive and can be saved or opened.

2. Devices on different networks

Phones on mobile data, a VPN or iCloud Private Relay put devices on different networks, so they don't see each other automatically. Pair them once:

  1. Tap the link button at the top and choose Create a pairing code.
  2. On the other device, enter the 6‑digit code, scan the QR code with the camera, or open the shared link.
  3. From then on the two devices find each other on any network. You can unpair them in the same window.

3. Public rooms for several devices

In the pairing window, create a public room. Share its 5‑letter code or link, and any number of devices can join from any network. Good for classrooms, meetings and family photo swaps. Add a password before creating the room to keep strangers out.

4. Share a file by link

In the ⋯ menu at the top, tap Share files by link, choose files and how long the link should work. The files are encrypted on your device, uploaded, and you get a link and a QR code that anyone can open, even later and on any device. See sending a file with a link for details.

5. Send text and links

Right-click a device (or long-press on a touch screen) and choose Send message: the chat with that device opens, and you type the text, a link or something you copied in the box at the bottom (see Chat with a device below). Messages you receive are read in the same chat: when one arrives, its chat opens by itself with a short sound. If you are busy with something else, a small banner at the top shows who sent it and the first words instead: tap it to open the chat. You can also paste into the page to send what is on your clipboard.

6. Received files

Tap the download button at the top to see the files you received in the last 7 days and save them again. Several files can be saved at once as one ZIP file. iPhone HEIC photos can be saved as JPG.

7. Install OmniBeam as an app

On Android and on computers with Chrome or Edge, OmniBeam offers Install at the bottom of the page. On iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. Installed on Android, OmniBeam also appears in the Share menu of your gallery and other apps.

8. Windows: the OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store

Get the free OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store. It opens in its own window, starts from the Start menu or the taskbar, and appears in the Windows Share menu: right-click files in File Explorer, choose Share and then OmniBeam. In Chrome or Edge, the OmniBeam extension sends the page, a link, an image or selected text with a right-click. See OmniBeam for Windows for the steps.

9. Faster transfers on the same Wi‑Fi

Tap ⚡ Faster Wi‑Fi transfers at the bottom and allow the microphone once. Nothing is recorded; browsers only share the device's local network address with sites that have this permission, and with it the devices can connect directly over your Wi‑Fi instead of through the router's internet connection. Tap the network ID next to your device name to run a connection check.

10. Folders, queue, cancel and auto-accept

  • Folders: drag a folder onto a device, or hover over it and choose Send folder. It arrives as a ZIP with the same folders inside.
  • Queue and cancel: files you send while a transfer runs wait in line. Tap ✕ on a device to stop. With several devices visible, Send to all my devices sends to all of them.
  • Auto-accept: in the link window, turn on Auto-accept for a paired device to receive its files without being asked.
  • Trusted devices: in the link window, tick Trusted next to your own remembered devices. They always show on the main screen, greyed out while offline, and their files arrive without asking.
  • Original or smaller photos: photos go in their original size. For a slow connection choose Smaller under Photos in the More… window or in the chat, or in Settings under Photo size when sending: big photos are then scaled to 2048 px on the longest side (JPG), the right way up, and OmniBeam shows how much it saved. GIFs, pictures with transparent parts and photos that are already small are sent as they are. Settings can also make videos smaller (720p). The originals on your device never change, and the choice is remembered.
  • Interrupted transfer: if a page reloads or the connection drops, send the same files again within a day and only the missing part is sent.
  • Language: choose English, العربية, Français, Español, Türkçe, Deutsch, Português, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, Русский, 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어, Italiano or اردو at the bottom of the page.

11. Send a link by email or text message

In the ⋯ menu at the top, tap Send to email or phone (on a computer it says Send by email). Type an email address, or a phone number on a phone, and choose the files. OmniBeam encrypts and uploads them, then opens your email or messages app with the download link already written, so you only press Send. You can also fill in Send to (optional) when you share files by link. Text messages work on phones; computers use email.

12. Link options: up to 7 days, download limit and PIN

  • How long: a link works for 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days or up to 7 days. After that the files are deleted from the server.
  • Downloads allowed: choose no limit, or 1, 3, 5 or 10 downloads. When the limit is reached, the link stops working. Under Your links you see how many downloads are used and how many are left.
  • PIN: add a PIN of 4 to 8 digits. The person then needs the link and the PIN to download. Tell them the PIN another way, for example on a call, not in the same message. After 10 wrong tries the link locks for good.

13. Continue a dropped send on another device

If a send to one of your paired devices stops halfway, for example because that phone left the Wi‑Fi, OmniBeam shows a card a few seconds later that offers to send the rest to another of your paired devices. Tap Send to … and only the files that did not arrive are sent there. If the first device comes back, the transfer continues by itself and the card goes away.

14. History, gallery and Save all

Tap the download button at the top to open History. It has three tabs:

  • Received: files and messages from the last 7 days, ready to save again.
  • Gallery: the photos and videos among them as thumbnails. Tap one to see it full size, then swipe or use the arrow keys for the next one. Save all saves every photo and video at once: as one ZIP on a computer, or to Photos on iPhone and iPad.
  • Sent: what this device sent, with Send again (see the next step).

When two or more photos or videos arrive together, the window that shows them also has a Gallery button. On iPhone and iPad, tap Save to Photos and then “Save Image” or “Save Video”, so photos go into the Photos app instead of Files.

15. Sent history, Send again and export

The Sent tab in History lists what you sent in the last 7 days: to which device, which files, and whether they arrived, were declined or failed. Tap Send again to send the same files to the same device, or pick another device if that one isn't there. After the page reloads, Send again asks you to choose the files once more, because browsers don't keep them. Export downloads the list as a spreadsheet (CSV) for Excel or Google Sheets, and Clear all empties the tab.

16. Team rooms for family or office

A team room is a fixed link for a group of devices. Tap the link button at the top, type a name like Family or Office under Team rooms and tap Create. Share the link or show its QR code. Every device that opens the link once stays in the room, on any network, every time OmniBeam opens, with no codes and no pairing again. Cards of team members show the team name, and you can turn on auto-accept for a team. A team room holds up to 50 devices, and you can leave it in the same window.

17. Your device name, picture and theme

Tap your device name at the bottom, or Change name and picture in Settings, to give this device a name and an emoji picture. Other devices see both on its card. Random name picks a new name for you. In Settings you can also choose the Theme: Automatic follows your device, or pick Light or Dark.

18. Clipboard sync and speed test

  • Clipboard sync: pair your devices, then turn on Sync the clipboard with my paired devices in Settings on both. Text you copy on one lands on the other's clipboard while OmniBeam is open. If the browser blocks clipboard access, tap Send my clipboard now.
  • Speed test: tap Test speed in the ⋯ menu or in Settings to see how fast this device and another one can really transfer, and which way the files go.

19. Photo, voice note, screen sharing and secret notes

  • More…: hover over a device and choose More…, or long-press it on a phone, to see everything you can send to it.
  • Take a photo: the camera opens right in OmniBeam. Tap Take photo, then Send. Turn on Document scan for sharp black-and-white pages and whiteboards.
  • Voice note: hold the round button while you speak and let go to stop. Listen to it, then send it.
  • Share my screen: on a computer, choose Share my screen and pick a window or the whole screen. The other device, even a tablet or a TV browser, taps Watch and sees it live, with nothing to install. Nothing is recorded.
  • Secret note: send a password or code that is shown once and never saved. In Share files by link, Secret note instead makes a link that opens only once.
  • Preview: in Received files and History, tap Preview to look at a PDF, picture, audio, text or code file before saving it.

20. Request files from anyone

In the ⋯ menu at the top, tap Request files. Write what you need if you like (for example “the photos from Saturday”), choose how long the link works, how many uploads it takes and an optional PIN, then send the link or QR code to anyone. They open it on any phone or computer, with no app, account or pairing, pick files and tap Send. The files are encrypted on their device and arrive on yours, in Received files. If OmniBeam is closed when they send, the files wait on the server and arrive the next time you open it (with notifications on, you get one). Your request links shows how many uploads each link got.

21. Show photos on a TV

Open omnibeam.site/tv in your smart TV’s browser (or on a computer connected to the TV), or choose TV mode in the ⋯ menu. The TV shows a big QR code: scan it with your phone’s camera, and OmniBeam opens on the phone already paired with the TV. Tap the TV and choose photos or videos; they arrive by themselves and play as a full-screen slideshow, a new photo every 8 seconds. With the remote, the arrows move between the buttons and OK chooses. In the slideshow, ◀ and ▶ change photos (in a video they skip 10 seconds), ▲ and ▼ go to the previous or next one, OK pauses, and Back opens the photo wall, a grid of everything received; Back again returns to the QR code. Music sent to the TV plays in the background, messages show in big letters (a link also as a QR code to scan), and an iPhone photo the TV can’t read says how to send it. Slideshow settings change the speed, the order, the fade and a slow zoom. Photos stay after the TV page reloads, until you choose Clear photos. A paired phone can be the remote: tap the TV, then Remote. After a few quiet minutes, a clock screen saver moves slowly around the screen. Phones on the same Wi‑Fi see the TV without scanning (the TV asks before taking their files). TV mode only receives: it never sends anything. Each QR code pairs one phone, and a new code appears right away for the next one.

Password for the TV: choose Set a PIN on the TV screen and type 4 to 8 digits. After scanning the QR code, the phone asks for the PIN, and only phones that type it can pair. With a PIN on, the TV turns away files from phones that have not paired, even on the same Wi‑Fi. The PIN never leaves the TV or the phone. Like every OmniBeam transfer, photos sent to the TV are end-to-end encrypted.

22. A “Send me files” box on your website

In Request files, choose 1 year (for a website), make the link, tap Put this on your website and copy the code into your website or blog (an HTML or Embed block). Visitors send you files right there, end-to-end encrypted, and they arrive here like any upload to the link. On WordPress you can use the free OmniBeam plugin instead: a block and a [omnibeam_box] shortcode. How it works.

23. Telegram

In Settings, tap Link Telegram, then tap Start in the chat with the OmniBeam bot. Files, photos, videos, voice notes and text you send to the bot arrive on this device, and Send to Telegram in the ⋯ menu sends files and text to that chat. Telegram lets bots receive files up to 20 MB and send files up to 50 MB. Files that go through Telegram are not end-to-end encrypted, because Telegram sees them.

24. Chat with a device

Choose Chat in a device's More… window, or Chat next to a paired device in the link window. You see everything you sent to that device and received from it in the last 7 days, messages and files, in time order like a messenger: what came in on one side, what you sent on the other. Received photos show a small picture, and every received file has Save (and Preview where it can be previewed). Type in the box at the bottom to send a message, or tap the paper clip to send files. A paired device that is offline gets them as soon as it opens OmniBeam. When something new arrives, a small number appears on the device's card: tap it to open the chat. The chat is made from the history on this device; nothing extra is saved and nothing goes to our server. Every Send message opens this chat: it is the one place where messages are written and read. When a message arrives, its chat opens by itself with a short sound (and a buzz on phones); if you are busy with something else, a banner shows instead, and you get a notification when the chat isn't on screen. The speaker button at the top turns the sound off (also in Settings, Message sound). The bin button clears the chat with that device from this device only, with a few seconds to undo.

25. Scan to PDF

  1. Choose Scan to PDF in a device's More… window. The camera opens in OmniBeam (or tap Use the camera app).
  2. Hold the page flat in good light, with its four corners in the picture, and tap Take page. Take as many pages as you need, then tap Done.
  3. OmniBeam finds the edges of each page and straightens it. If a page is cut wrongly, tap it and drag the four corners onto the corners of the page.
  4. Choose Colour or Black and white (sharp black text on white, good for documents and receipts), move pages earlier or later, or delete one.
  5. Tap Send to …. The PDF is made on your phone, nothing is uploaded to make it, and it is sent like any other file. You can also Share by link or Save PDF.

26. Supporter perks

OmniBeam is free, with no ads, and nothing is locked. If you donate with the ♥ button, you can get a supporter code as a thank-you. Type it in Settings under Supporter and tap Unlock: other devices then see a ♥ on your device's card, and you get four extra colour themes (Ocean, Forest, Sunset and Rose) and extra pictures for your device in Change name and picture. The code works on all your devices, and keeps working offline.

27. Event photo album

In the ⋯ menu, tap Event photo album. Give it a name (for example “Sara and Omar's wedding”), choose how long it stays open (1, 3, 7 or 30 days) and the photo size, and tap Make album. You get one link and QR code: show it, send it, or tap Print poster to put it on the tables. Guests scan it with the phone camera, add their photos and videos and see everyone's, with no app, account or pairing. Photos are encrypted on the guest's phone, and the key is in the part of the link after #, which never reaches our server.

Tap Open album (it is also under Your albums) to see every photo, save them all, delete any photo, or stop new photos. Guests see right away when the album stops taking photos, and they can still look at and save the ones in it. A photo deleted by you, or by the guest who added it, leaves every open album page within a few seconds. When the album ends, it is deleted from the server.

28. Share a folder and browse it from your phone

On a computer, tap Share a folder in the ⋯ menu, choose a folder and, under Who can look, tick your paired phone. On the phone, tap the computer, then More… and Browse files: you see the folders and files on the computer, can search them, and take one file or many. They come straight from the computer, encrypted, and nothing is uploaded. Keep the tab open on the computer while you need it. Hidden files are not shown.

29. Send to all my devices

When two or more of your devices are on screen, a Send to all my devices button appears. Tap it, untick any device you want to leave out, and send files or a message to all of them at once. You can also drop files on the button. Paired devices that are offline (greyed out) get it as soon as they open OmniBeam, so keep this page open.

30. Ring a device to find it

Lost your phone somewhere in the house? On another device, choose Ring this device in its More… window or at the top of its chat. The phone plays a loud sound (and vibrates) until someone taps Stop. If OmniBeam is closed on it but notifications are on, it gets a notification and rings when it opens; otherwise it rings if it opens OmniBeam in the next 2 minutes.

31. Links, pinned notes and invite cards

  • Open links: a link sent from another device shows Open link: one tap opens it. In Settings, under Links, you can let links from your paired devices open by themselves (the browser may ask you to allow pop-ups).
  • Pinned note: in a chat, tap the pin button (Pin a note) to keep a note at the top, like a Wi‑Fi password or an address. It stays on this device until you unpin it, even after the chat is cleared.
  • Invite a friend: in the ⋯ menu, tap Invite a friend to make a picture with a QR code that opens OmniBeam, in square or story size. Share it in a chat or a story, or tap Save picture.

32. App lock and Keep history

  • App lock: in Settings, under Privacy, turn on App lock and choose a PIN of 4 to 8 digits. OmniBeam then asks for it before it opens, so people who use the device after you can't see your history, chats or devices. You can also unlock with fingerprint, face or Windows Hello, choose when it locks again, or tap Lock now in the ⋯ menu. Files still arrive while it's locked, and notifications hide their text. If you forget the PIN, Forgot your PIN? removes the lock together with the history, chats and remembered devices on this device.
  • Keep history: choose how long received files and messages, the Sent list and clipboard history stay on this device: 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days (the default) or 30 days. Older ones are deleted by themselves. Pinned notes stay.

33. Move to a new phone

In Settings, under Device, tap Save backup file and choose a PIN or password for the file. The file holds your paired devices, settings, pinned notes and chats, encrypted with that password. Received files aren't in it. Send it to the new phone (with OmniBeam itself), open OmniBeam there, tap Open backup file and type the password. Everything is back, and the new phone already knows your other devices.

34. Buttons in notifications

With notifications on, a notification for a message has Reply, one for a file has Save, and one for a ringing device has Stop. Where the browser lets you type in the notification, your reply goes straight back; elsewhere, Reply opens the chat ready to type. While App lock is on, notifications only let you open OmniBeam or stop a ring.

35. Smaller videos, made quickly

In Settings, under Photo size when sending, you can make videos smaller too: 720p, or 480p with Smallest. Chrome, Edge and Safari make a one-minute clip smaller in a few seconds, right on your device; in other browsers it takes about as long as the video plays. The original video on your device doesn't change.

36. Text size, contrast and Liquid Glass

In Settings, under Device: Text size makes all text Large or Largest; Contrast set to High gives solid colours with clear edges (Automatic follows your device's setting); and Style switches between Classic and Liquid Glass, a see-through glass look, in light and dark. OmniBeam also works with screen readers, the keyboard and Windows high contrast.

37. Auto-send a folder, clipboard history and Firefox

  • Auto-send a folder: on a computer with Chrome or Edge, or in the Windows app, choose a folder in Settings under Auto-send a folder, and the device to send to. New files you put in that folder go to that device by themselves while OmniBeam is open. Turn on Auto-accept for the computer on the phone, so they arrive without a tap.
  • Clipboard history: with Clipboard sync on, tick Keep the last 20 in History to copy an earlier text again later.
  • Firefox: the OmniBeam extension is also on Firefox Add-ons, next to the Chrome Web Store version.

Troubleshooting

  • Devices don't appear: check both are on the same Wi‑Fi and not using a VPN, or pair them with a code.
  • “Sending via server” or “internet” route on the same Wi‑Fi: see how to fix slow transfers on the same Wi‑Fi. It still works, just slower, and stays end-to-end encrypted.
  • Transfer stops when the screen turns off: phones pause web pages when the screen locks or you switch apps. Keep OmniBeam open and the screen on until the transfer finishes; OmniBeam asks the phone to stay awake and shows a reminder on the card. If the screen does turn off, open OmniBeam again and the transfer continues by itself where it stopped.
