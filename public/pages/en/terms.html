Questions and answers

What do these terms cover?

They cover your use of OmniBeam: sending files and text between devices, pairing devices, joining public rooms and sharing files by link. The answers below summarise the main points in plain words; if anything here seems to differ from the terms above, the terms above apply.

Who is responsible for the files I send?

You are. OmniBeam only carries the files from one device to another, encrypted, and cannot see what they contain. Only send files you have the right to share, and never use OmniBeam to send anything illegal or harmful.

Can I use OmniBeam for work?

Yes. You can use it to move your own work files between your devices or to share files with colleagues, as long as this is allowed by your employer’s rules and the files are yours to share.

What happens to files shared by link?

They are stored encrypted only until the link expires or until you delete the link, and are then removed. Anyone who has the link can download the files, so share it carefully.

Is there a guarantee that transfers always work?

OmniBeam is provided as it is, free of charge. A great deal of work goes into making transfers reliable, but networks and devices differ, so keep your own copies of important files until they have arrived.

Can the terms change?

Yes. When the terms change, the date of this page is updated. Using OmniBeam after a change means you accept the new terms.

Are there limits on how I can use OmniBeam?

Use it fairly: do not try to overload or break the service, do not send spam or malware, and do not use public rooms to harass other people. Accounts are not needed, but abuse of the service can be blocked.

Where can I ask a question about the terms?

Send a message through the feedback link on the About page. For questions about how your data is handled, see the privacy policy, which explains exactly what is and is not stored.

Can I send to several devices at once?

Yes. With “Send to everyone”, the same files go to every device that has OmniBeam open in one step. For a group you can also create a public room: everyone joins with the same short code and can send files to each other.
