How to transfer files from Android to a PC without a USB cable

USB cables, drivers and "File transfer" mode are a hassle. With OmniBeam you send files from any Android phone to a Windows PC, Mac or Chromebook over Wi‑Fi, straight from Chrome.

Steps

  1. Open omnibeam.site on the PC and in Chrome on the phone.
  2. Wait a moment until each one shows the other. Not showing up? Use a pairing code: tap the link button on the PC, create a code and type it on the phone.
  3. On the phone, tap the PC and pick photos, videos or any file.
  4. Accept on the PC and save. Several files can be saved together as a ZIP.

Even quicker: the Android share menu

Add OmniBeam to your home screen (Chrome menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app). After that, OmniBeam appears in the share menu of the Gallery, Files and most other apps: select photos, tap Share, choose OmniBeam and pick the PC.

Tips for big videos

  • Keep the phone's screen on and the page open until the transfer finishes.
  • On a slow connection, choose Smaller photos and videos in Settings (in the ⋯ menu at the top): photos are scaled to 2048 px and videos re-recorded at 720p before sending.
  • Transfers on the same Wi‑Fi are fastest. If the progress card says the route is "internet" or "server", see how to fix slow transfers on the same Wi‑Fi.
  • If you send from your Android phone often, install the free OmniBeam app for Windows and open it from the Start menu whenever a file is on its way.

It also works when the phone and the PC are on different networks, for example with the phone on mobile data: pair them with a code and the files travel over an encrypted connection. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud folder first, so a big video starts arriving right away.

Questions and answers

Do I need to install anything on the PC?

No. Open omnibeam.site in any browser on the PC. If you send files often, the free OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store keeps it ready in the background.

Can I share straight from the Android gallery?

Yes. Install OmniBeam as an app from the browser menu, and it appears in the Android share menu, so you can send a photo directly from the gallery.

What if the PC is on Ethernet and the phone on Wi‑Fi?

That is fine as long as both are on the same home network. If they are on different networks, pair them once with a code.

Is it faster than a USB cable?

On a good Wi‑Fi network it is close, and for a handful of photos it is quicker because you do not have to find a cable or unlock the phone’s file mode.

Where are the files saved on Windows?

They are saved to your Downloads folder, or wherever your browser saves downloads. Several files can be saved together as one ZIP.

Can the PC send files back to the phone?

Yes. Click the phone in OmniBeam on the PC, choose the files, and accept them on the phone. With the older OmniBeam tray app you can also right-click a file in File Explorer and send it to the phone.
