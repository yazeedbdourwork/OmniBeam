Send a file with a link, encrypted

When the other person isn't online at the same moment, or you don't want to pair devices, share the files by link. They are encrypted on your device before they leave it.

Steps

  1. Open omnibeam.site, then tap Share files by link in the ⋯ menu at the top.
  2. Choose how long the link should work (1 hour, 1 day or 3 days) and, if you like, “Delete after the first download”.
  3. Choose the files. They are encrypted and uploaded, and you get a link and a QR code.
  4. Send the link by WhatsApp, email or any app. Whoever opens it can download the files on any device.

How private is it?

The files and their names are encrypted in your browser with a random key. The key is the part of the link after “#”, which browsers never send to a server, so OmniBeam stores only data it can't read. Treat the link like the file itself: anyone who has it can download.

Limits

  • Links have a size limit, shown when you create one.
  • Files are deleted automatically when the link expires. You can also delete a link yourself at any time from the same window.
  • For very large files, send directly to a device instead: direct transfers have no size limit.

Questions and answers

When is a link better than a direct transfer?

Use a link when the other person is not online at the same moment, when you want to send the same file to several people, or when you cannot pair devices. For very large files to your own devices, a direct transfer is faster and has no size limit.

Can I see whether the file was downloaded?

You can choose “Delete after the first download”, so the link stops working once it has been used. You can also delete the link yourself at any time from the same window.

Does the other person need OmniBeam?

No. They only open the link in a browser on any device and download the files. Nothing has to be installed and no account is needed.

Can OmniBeam see my files?

No. The files are encrypted in your browser before they are uploaded, and the key stays in the part of the link after “#”, which is never sent to the server.

How long does a link stay available?

You choose when you create it: 1 hour, 1 day or 3 days. After that time the files are deleted automatically and the link stops working.

Can I send several files with one link?

Yes. Choose as many files as you like, and the person who opens the link sees them all and can download them one by one or together.

Can I share the link as a QR code?

Yes. Every link comes with a QR code, so someone next to you can scan it with their phone camera and download straight away.
