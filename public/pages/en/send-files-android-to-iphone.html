How to send files from Android to iPhone

Android's Quick Share and Apple's AirDrop don't talk to each other. OmniBeam does: it runs in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone, with nothing to install.

Steps

  1. Open omnibeam.site on both phones.
  2. On the same Wi‑Fi they appear on each other's screen. Otherwise, tap the link button on one phone, create a code and enter it (or scan the QR code) on the other.
  3. On the Android phone, tap the iPhone and choose the photos or files.
  4. Tap Accept on the iPhone, then save the photos to Photos or the files to the Files app.

It works the other way round too: tap the Android phone on the iPhone to send from iPhone to Android.

Send from the Android Share menu

In Chrome on Android, choose Install (or Add to Home screen) from the menu. OmniBeam then appears in the Share menu of the Gallery and other apps: share, pick the iPhone, done.

Is it private?

Every transfer has to be accepted on the receiving phone. Files go directly between the phones whenever possible and are always encrypted end to end, also when they pass through the OmniBeam server.

Questions and answers

Why can’t I just use Bluetooth between Android and iPhone?

iPhones do not accept file transfers over Bluetooth from Android phones, and AirDrop only works between Apple devices. That is why sending between the two usually needs an app or a web page that both can open.

Will photos lose quality?

No. OmniBeam sends the original file, so photos and videos arrive exactly as they were taken, unlike messaging apps that compress them.

Where do the files go on the iPhone?

Photos and videos can be saved straight to the Photos app with the share sheet. Other files, such as PDFs or documents, are saved to the Files app, where you can move them to any folder.

Can I send from iPhone back to Android?

Yes. The same page works in both directions: tap the Android phone on the iPhone and choose the files.

What if the two phones are on different networks?

Pair them once with a 6‑digit code or by scanning the QR code. After that they find each other even when one is on mobile data and the other is on Wi‑Fi.

Is there a size limit?

Direct transfers have no size limit, so you can send long videos or many files at once. Keep both screens on until the transfer ends for the best speed.

Do I need to install an app on the iPhone?

No. Safari is enough. If you want quicker access, add omnibeam.site to the home screen from the share menu, and it opens like an app.

Can I send contacts or notes as text?

Yes. Paste any text, a link or a note into OmniBeam and send it; it appears on the other phone, where you can copy it.
