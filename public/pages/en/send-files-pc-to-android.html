How to send files from a PC to an Android phone without a cable

Short answer: open omnibeam.site on the PC and on the Android phone, click the phone on the PC, choose the files and tap Accept on the phone. The files go over Wi‑Fi in full quality, with no USB cable, no app and no account.

Step by step

  1. On the PC, open omnibeam.site in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  2. On the Android phone, open omnibeam.site in Chrome or any other browser. On the same Wi‑Fi, each device shows the other after a moment.
  3. On the PC, click the phone and choose the files, or drag them onto it. You can pick many files, or a whole folder.
  4. On the phone, tap Accept. The files are saved to the phone's Downloads, and photos and videos can be opened in the Gallery.

If the PC and the phone are on different networks

When the phone is on mobile data, or the PC is on a work network or a VPN, they won't see each other automatically. On the PC, click the link button and create a pairing code, then scan the QR code with the phone's camera or type the 6‑digit code. They stay paired from then on, so next time they find each other on any network.

Faster ways for every day

  • Right-click a file on Windows and use Send to, with the free OmniBeam tray app or the Microsoft Store app.
  • Install OmniBeam on the phone from Chrome's menu (Add to Home screen). It then appears in Android's Share menu too, for sending the other way.
  • Mark the PC as a Trusted device on the phone and turn on Auto‑accept, so files from your own PC arrive without a tap.
  • Send text, links and copied passwords through the chat with the phone instead of emailing them to yourself.

Why not a USB cable?

A cable works, but Android often opens it in charging-only mode, Windows has to find the phone's storage, and you need the right cable at hand. Over Wi‑Fi, OmniBeam is usually just as quick for photos, documents and APK files, and it also works when the phone is in another room or on another network. For very large videos, put both devices on the same Wi‑Fi for the best speed.

Everything is encrypted end to end on the PC, and only your phone can open the files. Nothing is stored on a server.

Questions and answers

Do I need to install an app on Android?

No. Open omnibeam.site in the phone's browser. Installing it to the home screen is optional and only makes it quicker to open.

Where do the files go on the phone?

Into the Downloads folder, like any download. Photos and videos also show up in the Gallery or Google Photos on most phones.

Is there a size limit?

No. OmniBeam sets no limit on direct sends, so you can send large videos, ZIP files and whole folders. Keep the phone's screen on during a long transfer.

Can I send a whole folder from the PC?

Yes. Drag the folder onto the phone, or use the folder option when choosing files. The files arrive with their names, and you can save them all at once.

Does it work with Samsung, Xiaomi, Pixel and other phones?

Yes. It works on any Android phone or tablet with a recent browser, and on Android TV as well.

Is it the same from a Mac or a Chromebook?

Yes. The steps are the same on any computer, because OmniBeam runs in the browser.
