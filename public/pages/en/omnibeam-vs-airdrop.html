OmniBeam vs AirDrop: the same idea on every device

AirDrop is the easiest way to move files between Apple devices. The moment a Windows PC or an Android phone is involved, it stops working. OmniBeam brings the same idea to every device, in the browser.

When AirDrop is better

If every device you own is made by Apple, keep using AirDrop. It is built in, works without any internet connection and is very fast between an iPhone and a Mac.

When OmniBeam is better

  • You have an iPhone and a Windows PC, or an Android phone and a Mac: this is exactly the "AirDrop for Windows" people look for.
  • The other device is at home and you are at work: paired devices see each other over the internet.
  • You want to send to a friend who isn't online right now: share an encrypted link instead.
  • You use a shared or work computer where you can't install apps.

How to switch in one minute

Open omnibeam.site on both devices, on the same Wi‑Fi. They appear on each other's screen; tap the other device and pick files. On iPhone, add OmniBeam to the Home Screen so it opens like an app. See how to send files from iPhone to PC for the details.

Questions and answers

Is OmniBeam as fast as AirDrop?

Between two devices on the same Wi‑Fi, both send directly from one device to the other, so the speed is similar and depends mostly on your network. AirDrop can also use a direct Wi‑Fi link without a router, which helps when there is no network at all; OmniBeam needs both devices to be online.

Can OmniBeam replace AirDrop on my iPhone?

If you also have Windows or Android devices, yes for those: OmniBeam works between the iPhone and any of them. Between two Apple devices, AirDrop is built in and works well, so many people keep both.

Does OmniBeam work when the devices are far apart?

Yes. AirDrop only works between devices near each other. OmniBeam can send between devices on different networks after you pair them once with a 6‑digit code or a QR code.

What about privacy?

Both encrypt transfers. OmniBeam encrypts every file end to end on your device and does not store it on the way.

Can I send to an Android phone from my Mac?

Not with AirDrop. With OmniBeam you can: open omnibeam.site on the Mac and on the Android phone, and the Android phone appears as a device you can send to, just like an Apple device in AirDrop.

Which one should I use day to day?

If all your devices are made by Apple, AirDrop is already built in and works well. As soon as one Windows PC, Android phone or Chromebook joins the mix, OmniBeam covers the gaps with the same tap-and-send idea.
