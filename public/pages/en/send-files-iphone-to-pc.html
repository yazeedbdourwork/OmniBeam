How to send files from iPhone to a Windows PC without a cable

AirDrop doesn't work with Windows, and cables, iTunes and emailing yourself are slow. With OmniBeam you send photos, videos and documents from iPhone to PC in the browser, in full quality.

On the same Wi‑Fi

  1. On the PC, open omnibeam.site in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  2. On the iPhone, open omnibeam.site in Safari. After a moment, each device shows the other one.
  3. On the iPhone, tap the PC and choose Photo Library or Choose Files.
  4. Accept on the PC. The files go straight across your Wi‑Fi and you can save them, or several at once as a ZIP.

On different networks

If the iPhone is on mobile data or a VPN, the devices won't see each other automatically. Tap the link button on the PC, create a pairing code, and scan the QR code with the iPhone's camera. They stay paired from then on.

Tips

  • iPhone photos are often HEIC, which Windows can't always open. OmniBeam can save them as JPG.
  • Keep Safari open on the iPhone until the transfer finishes; locking the screen pauses it.
  • For the fastest Wi‑Fi transfers, tap ⚡ Faster Wi‑Fi transfers on the iPhone and allow the microphone once (nothing is recorded).
  • Pin OmniBeam to the iPhone home screen: Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • On the PC, the free OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store keeps OmniBeam one click away on the taskbar.

Photos taken in HEIC format can be turned into JPG on the way, so they open on any Windows PC. Videos and documents are sent as they are, in full quality.

OmniBeam is free and sends files from iPhone to a PC without a cable or iTunes.

Questions and answers

Do I need iTunes or iCloud?

No. OmniBeam does not use iTunes, iCloud or a cable. Open omnibeam.site in Safari on the iPhone and in any browser on the PC, and send directly.

Can I send many photos at once?

Yes. Select as many photos and videos as you like in the iPhone’s picker. On the PC you can save them one by one or all together as a ZIP.

Will my HEIC photos open on Windows?

Yes. You can choose to save iPhone HEIC photos as JPG, which every Windows app can open.

What should I do if the transfer stops?

Keep the iPhone screen on while it sends, because iOS pauses the browser when the screen locks. If it does stop, OmniBeam continues automatically when the iPhone wakes up.

How do I pick photos from the Photos app?

Tap the PC in OmniBeam and choose Photo Library in the menu that opens. You can select many photos and videos at once, and they are sent in their original quality.

Does the PC need to be on the same Wi‑Fi?

No. On the same Wi‑Fi it appears automatically; otherwise pair the iPhone and the PC once with a 6‑digit code or the QR code.
