How to send photos and videos without losing quality

WhatsApp, Messenger and most chat apps compress pictures and videos to save data, so what arrives looks blurry and has lost its details. To keep the original, send the file itself instead of a chat message.

Send the original file with OmniBeam

  1. Open omnibeam.site on both devices (same Wi‑Fi, or paired with a code).
  2. Tap the other device and pick the photos or videos.
  3. Accept on the other device. The files arrive byte for byte as they left: same resolution, same quality, same date and camera details.

Make sure Photo size when sending is set to Original in Settings (it is, unless you changed it).

iPhone photos on Windows and Android

iPhones save photos as HEIC, which many Windows PCs and Android phones can't open. When an iPhone sends to one of them, OmniBeam can send a JPG copy instead, at high quality, so the photo opens everywhere.

When smaller is better

On a slow mobile connection, a smaller file that arrives now can beat a perfect one that takes an hour. Choose Smaller for those moments, in the More… window or in Settings: photos are scaled to 2048 pixels (videos can go to 720p), OmniBeam shows how much it saved, and the originals on your phone stay untouched.

Sharing with someone far away

If the other person can't open OmniBeam at the same time, share the files by link instead. They are encrypted on your device and the link expires on its own.

Questions and answers

Which apps reduce the quality of my photos?

Most messaging apps, including WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram direct messages, compress photos and videos to save data. Email often has a size limit that forces you to shrink large videos.

Does OmniBeam keep the original details?

Yes. The original file is sent, including its resolution and, where the file has them, details such as the date the photo was taken.

Can I send HEIC photos?

HEIC photos are sent as they are, and you can choose to save them as JPG on Windows or Android so they open everywhere.

Is there a limit on how many I can send?

No. Select as many photos and videos as you like, or add more while a transfer is running.

How do I check that nothing was compressed?

Compare the file size and the resolution on both devices. With OmniBeam they are identical, and OmniBeam also checks each file after the transfer to confirm it arrived intact.

Where are the photos saved on an iPhone?

Choose Save to Photos when the transfer ends, and they go straight into the Photos app, in full quality.

Can I send a whole album?

Yes. Select all the photos and videos from the album in the picker, and they are sent together. On a computer, you can save them as a ZIP to keep them in one place.
