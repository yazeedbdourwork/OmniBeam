OmniBeam vs Snapdrop

Snapdrop made browser file sharing popular: open the site on two devices on the same Wi‑Fi and send. It was later taken over by LimeWire, and the site is now part of LimeWire's service. OmniBeam keeps the simple idea and adds what Snapdrop never had.

What stays the same

Like Snapdrop, OmniBeam needs no app and no sign-up, devices on the same network find each other by themselves, and files go directly from one device to the other with WebRTC.

What OmniBeam adds

  • Pairing: connect a phone on mobile data to a PC at home with a short code, once.
  • Resume: if the connection drops halfway through a big video, the transfer continues instead of starting over.
  • Text, clipboard sync between paired devices, and notifications when a file arrives.
  • Right-click "Send to OmniBeam" on Windows, Mac and Linux, and a browser extension.

Moving from Snapdrop

There is nothing to move: open omnibeam.site where you used to open Snapdrop. If you liked Snapdrop's open-source spirit, PairDrop is its community successor; see OmniBeam vs PairDrop.

If Snapdrop no longer works for you, OmniBeam is an alternative that works the same way in the browser.

Questions and answers

What happened to Snapdrop?

Snapdrop was a popular open-source page for sending files between devices on the same network. It was later taken over by LimeWire, and many users noticed changes and looked for alternatives that work the way the original did.

Does OmniBeam work like the original Snapdrop?

Yes for the basics: open the page on two devices on the same Wi‑Fi, they appear automatically, tap one and send. OmniBeam adds pairing across networks, encrypted links, resuming a transfer after a drop, and 15 languages.

Do I need an account?

No. Like the original Snapdrop, OmniBeam works without an account or sign-up.

Is it free?

Yes. Sending between devices is free, with no ads.

Does OmniBeam work across different networks?

Yes. The original Snapdrop only found devices on the same network. OmniBeam can pair devices once with a 6‑digit code or a QR code, and they then find each other even when one is on mobile data.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Every transfer is encrypted end to end on your device, also when the data has to pass through a relay because a direct connection is blocked.

Can I keep using the same habits?

Yes. If you used Snapdrop by opening a bookmark on each device, do the same with omnibeam.site. You can also add OmniBeam to your home screen so it opens like an app.
