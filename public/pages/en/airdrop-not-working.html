AirDrop not working? Quick fixes, and another way to send

AirDrop often fails without saying why: the other device doesn't show up, or the transfer hangs on "Waiting". Here are the usual fixes, and a way to send the files right now, even to devices that aren't Apple.

Quick fixes for AirDrop

  • Turn Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth on for both devices, and Personal Hotspot off.
  • Set AirDrop to Everyone for 10 Minutes on the receiving device.
  • Unlock both devices and keep them within a few metres of each other.
  • Turn off Focus or Do Not Disturb, and VPN apps, then try again.
  • Still nothing? Restart both devices.

Send the files anyway, in the browser

  1. Open omnibeam.site on both devices (Safari is fine).
  2. On the same Wi‑Fi they appear by themselves; otherwise pair them with a 6‑digit code.
  3. Tap the other device, pick photos, videos or files, and accept on the other side.

It works between iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Android, keeps photos and videos in full quality, and doesn't need Bluetooth. More in OmniBeam vs AirDrop.

Why does AirDrop fail so often? It needs Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi on both devices at once, and it only works between Apple devices. OmniBeam uses the network you already have, so it keeps working with Bluetooth off, across different networks and with devices that aren't Apple.

If the quick fixes above do not help, OmniBeam is another way to send the same files from the browser.

Questions and answers

Why does AirDrop not find the other device?

The most common reasons are Bluetooth or Wi‑Fi turned off on one device, AirDrop set to “Receiving Off” or “Contacts Only” when the sender is not in your contacts, or the other device being asleep. Personal Hotspot and some VPNs also block AirDrop, so turn them off while you try again.

Why does AirDrop stay on “Waiting”?

The receiving device usually has not accepted yet, or its screen turned off. Unlock it, keep it nearby and make sure it is set to receive from everyone for ten minutes. Restarting both devices clears most stuck transfers.

What if the other device is not an Apple device?

Then AirDrop can never work, because it is only built into iPhone, iPad and Mac. Use a cross-platform tool instead: open omnibeam.site on both devices and send the files from the browser.

Does OmniBeam need Bluetooth?

No. OmniBeam uses the network, not Bluetooth. Devices on the same Wi‑Fi find each other automatically, and devices on different networks can pair with a 6‑digit code.

Could my iPhone settings be the problem?

Yes. Screen Time restrictions can turn AirDrop off completely, and a work or school profile can block it. Check Settings, then Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions, and make sure AirDrop is allowed. Also check that both devices have a recent version of iOS or macOS, because AirDrop between very old and very new versions is less reliable.

Is there a way that never depends on AirDrop?

Yes. Because OmniBeam runs in the browser and uses your normal network connection, AirDrop settings, Bluetooth and Screen Time do not affect it. It is a dependable second option to keep in mind for the day AirDrop refuses to cooperate, and it works the same way with Windows and Android devices too.
