How to send files from a PC to an iPhone without iTunes

Getting a file from Windows onto an iPhone usually means iTunes, a cable or emailing it to yourself. OmniBeam does it in the browser on both devices, in full quality.

Steps

  1. On the PC, open omnibeam.site. On the iPhone, open the same address in Safari.
  2. On the same Wi‑Fi, the two devices show each other after a moment. On different networks, tap the link button on one device, create a pairing code and enter it (or scan the QR code) on the other.
  3. On the PC, click the iPhone and pick the files, or drag them onto it. A whole folder works too: it arrives as a ZIP.
  4. On the iPhone, tap Accept, then Save.

Where the files end up on the iPhone

  • Saved files go to the Files app, in the Downloads folder (On My iPhone or iCloud Drive).
  • To put a photo or video in the Photos app, open it in Files, tap the share button and choose Save Image or Save Video.
  • Missed one? The download button at the top of OmniBeam lists what you received in the last 7 days.

Make it one click next time

Pair the two devices once and mark the PC as Trusted in the iPhone's Pair screen. From then on they find each other on any network, and files from the PC arrive without asking. On the PC, the free OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store keeps OmniBeam one click away on the taskbar and in the Windows Share menu: see OmniBeam for Windows.

On the iPhone, received photos and videos can be saved straight to the Photos app, and other files go to the Files app. Add OmniBeam to the Home Screen from Safari to find it faster next time.

Questions and answers

Where do the files appear on the iPhone?

Safari asks where to save them. Photos and videos can go straight to the Photos app, and other files such as PDFs, music or documents are saved in the Files app.

Can I send a folder from the PC?

Yes. Drag a folder onto the iPhone in OmniBeam on the PC. The files arrive on the iPhone and can be saved together.

Does it work with an iPad?

Yes. The steps are the same on an iPad: open omnibeam.site in Safari and accept the files.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account, no app and no size limit for direct transfers.

Can I send music to the iPhone?

Yes. MP3 and other audio files are saved in the Files app, where you can play them, or open them in another app that plays music.

Why do I have to tap Accept on the iPhone?

For your safety, a device only receives files after you accept them. You can turn on Auto-accept for your own paired devices in the pairing window.

Can I send to several iPhones at once?

Yes. Use “Send to everyone” on the PC, and every device that has OmniBeam open receives the files.
