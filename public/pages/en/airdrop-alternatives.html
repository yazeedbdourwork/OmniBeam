The best AirDrop alternatives for Windows and Android

AirDrop is great until a Windows PC or an Android phone is involved. Here are the main ways to send files across all your devices, and when each one makes sense.

1. Browser-based sharing (OmniBeam, PairDrop, Snapdrop)

Open the same website on both devices and they find each other, much like AirDrop. Nothing to install, it works on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux, and files go directly between the devices when the network allows it.

OmniBeam adds pairing codes for different networks, end-to-end encryption when the direct path fails, resume after a dropped connection, links for people who aren't online, a Windows app in the Microsoft Store and a Chrome extension.

2. Quick Share (Android and Windows)

Google's Quick Share works well between Android phones and Windows PCs with Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi. It doesn't support iPhones, and the PC needs the Quick Share app.

3. LocalSend

A free, open-source app for every platform that sends files over your local network. Very fast, but it must be installed on each device and only works when both are on the same network.

4. Cloud links (Google Drive, OneDrive, WeTransfer)

Upload, then share a link. Good for sending to someone far away or for keeping a copy, but every file goes up to the internet and back down, which is slow for big videos, and the files sit on someone else's server.

5. Messaging apps

Quick for a photo or two, but most chat apps compress pictures and videos. See how to keep full quality.

Which one to choose

  • Mixed devices, nothing to install: a browser app such as OmniBeam.
  • Only Android and Windows: Quick Share.
  • Lots of large files on one network, every day: LocalSend, or OmniBeam in the browser on the same Wi‑Fi.
  • Someone who isn't online right now: a link. OmniBeam's links are encrypted on your device and expire on their own.

Side-by-side details are in Compare OmniBeam.

In short, the best AirDrop alternatives for Windows and Android are the ones that work in a browser, and OmniBeam is one of them.

Questions and answers

What should a good AirDrop alternative do?

It should work between any two devices you own, not only between Apple ones, and it should be quick to start. The best options also keep files private with end-to-end encryption, send photos and videos without shrinking them, and still work when the two devices are not on the same Wi‑Fi.

Do I need to install anything?

Not with OmniBeam: it runs in the browser, so it also works on a work laptop or a friend’s computer where you cannot install apps. Some other alternatives need an app on every device, which is fine at home but slower when you just want to send one file.

Which alternative is the fastest?

On the same Wi‑Fi, tools that connect the two devices directly are the fastest, because nothing has to be uploaded first. OmniBeam sends directly whenever it can, and falls back to an encrypted relay only when a direct connection is blocked.

Can I use more than one?

Yes. Many people keep AirDrop for Apple-to-Apple sharing and use OmniBeam for everything else, such as iPhone to Windows or Android to Mac.

What about sending to someone else’s phone?

That works too. Ask them to open omnibeam.site, or create a public room that you both join with the same short code. If they are not online now, send an encrypted link they can open later.
