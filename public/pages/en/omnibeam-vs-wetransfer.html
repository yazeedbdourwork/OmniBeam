OmniBeam vs WeTransfer

Short answer: use OmniBeam when the other device can be online at the same time, because the file goes straight across with no size limit and nothing to upload first. Use WeTransfer when you need to leave a very large file for someone to download days later.

Choose WeTransfer if

You need to hand over a very large project to a client who will download it later, perhaps days later, and you want a familiar download page with your branding. That is what WeTransfer is built for, and its paid plans keep big files online for a long time.

Choose OmniBeam if

  • You are moving files between your own phone and computer: a direct send is faster, because the file travels once instead of being uploaded and downloaded.
  • The file is bigger than WeTransfer's free plan allows, and the other person can open OmniBeam at the same time.
  • Privacy matters: with OmniBeam the file is encrypted on your device and only the receiving device can open it.
  • You don't want to wait for an upload to finish before the other person can start receiving.

How to send a big file with OmniBeam

  1. Open omnibeam.site on both devices.
  2. If they are on different networks, pair them once with the 6‑digit code or the QR code.
  3. Tap the other device, choose the files and wait for them to be accepted.
  4. If the other person isn't online, use Share files by link in the ⋯ menu instead, and send them the link.

WeTransfer's limits change from time to time, so check its current plans if you rely on a particular size or storage time. You can also read the step-by-step guide to sending large files without WeTransfer.

Questions and answers

Is OmniBeam a free alternative to WeTransfer?

Yes for most everyday uses. Direct sends are free with no size limit, and links are free too. For leaving huge files online for weeks, WeTransfer's paid plans are a better fit.

Which one is faster?

For a direct send, OmniBeam is usually faster, because the file goes once from your device to the other one. With WeTransfer it has to finish uploading before the other person can download it.

Which one is more private?

OmniBeam encrypts every transfer end to end on your device, so its server cannot read the files. WeTransfer encrypts files on the way and on its servers, but it holds the files and the keys.

Does the other person need an account?

No, for either one. With OmniBeam they open omnibeam.site or your link in any browser.

Can I send a 20 GB video with OmniBeam?

Yes, with a direct send. Keep both devices awake with the page open; if the connection drops, the transfer continues where it stopped.

Can I use both?

Yes. Many people use OmniBeam between their own devices and for quick sends, and WeTransfer when a client needs a download page that stays online.
