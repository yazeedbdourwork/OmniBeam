OmniBeam: frequently asked questions

OmniBeam is a free website for sending files, photos, videos and text between any two devices, such as an iPhone and a Windows PC. Open omnibeam.site on both devices and send. There is no app to install, no account, and every transfer is encrypted end to end.

The basics

What is OmniBeam?

OmniBeam is a browser-based way to send files between your devices, like AirDrop but for every brand. It works on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook, in Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox and other modern browsers.

Is OmniBeam free?

Yes. Sending files, links, chat and every other feature are free, with no account and no ads. People who want to support the project can donate and get a supporter badge with extra themes, but nothing is locked behind it.

Do I need to install an app?

No. OmniBeam runs in the web browser. If you like, you can add it to the home screen of a phone, install it from the Microsoft Store on Windows, or add the Chrome, Edge or Firefox extension, but the website alone does everything.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. Each device simply gets a name, which you can change.

How do I send a file?

Open omnibeam.site on both devices. On the same Wi‑Fi they show each other after a moment. Tap the other device, choose the files, and accept on the other device. The files go straight across.

Networks and devices

Do both devices have to be on the same Wi‑Fi?

No. On the same Wi‑Fi they find each other automatically. On different networks, such as a phone on mobile data, pair them once with a 6‑digit code or by scanning a QR code. Paired devices find each other from then on, wherever they are.

Can I send from iPhone to Windows or Android?

Yes. That is what OmniBeam is made for: iPhone to PC, Android to iPhone, Android to Mac, Mac to Windows and any other mix. The steps are the same on every device.

Does it work without internet?

The devices need a connection to omnibeam.site to find each other. On the same Wi‑Fi the file itself then goes directly over your local network.

Privacy and safety

Is OmniBeam safe?

Yes. Every transfer is encrypted end to end on the sending device, and only the receiving device can decrypt it. Files go directly between the devices whenever possible. When they can't, the encrypted data passes through a relay that cannot read it. Nothing from a live transfer is stored, and every file must be accepted on the receiving device.

Does OmniBeam keep my files?

No. Live transfers are never saved on a server. Only files you share by link, or that someone sends through a file request, are stored, encrypted, and they are deleted when the link expires.

Is OmniBeam open source?

Yes. The source code is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 license, so anyone can check how it works.

Files and limits

Is there a file size limit?

Not for direct sends: OmniBeam sets no limit, and in Chrome, Edge and Firefox large files are written straight to disk. Shared links have a size cap, shown in the app, because those files are stored until the link expires.

Are photos and videos sent in full quality?

Yes, by default they are sent as the original files. You can choose Smaller photos or Smaller videos when you want to save time or space, and iPhone HEIC photos can be saved as JPG.

What happens if the connection drops?

The transfer continues where it stopped instead of starting over, either on the direct link when it comes back or through the encrypted relay.

Can I send to someone who isn't online right now?

Yes. Share files by an encrypted link that works for 7 days by default, or up to 30 days. You can add a PIN and have OmniBeam email the link.

More than sending files

Can I chat or call with OmniBeam?

Yes. Paired devices can chat, send voice messages, start a group chat and make voice or video calls, all encrypted end to end. You can also share your screen, draw together on a shared whiteboard, and keep shared notes and to-do lists.

How do I know I paired with the right device?

In More… on a device, tap Safety code. Both devices show the same 12 digits when they are connected straight to each other. If the codes are different, don't send anything and connect again.

Can OmniBeam back up my phone's photos to my computer?

Yes. Photo backup sends new photos from your phone to a paired computer and skips the ones it sent before. You can turn on Back up new photos by themselves, so it happens whenever OmniBeam is open on both.

Compared with other tools

How is OmniBeam different from AirDrop?

AirDrop only works between Apple devices that are close together. OmniBeam works between every brand and across different networks, in the browser. See OmniBeam vs AirDrop.

How is it different from WeTransfer?

WeTransfer uploads your files to its servers first. OmniBeam sends them directly from device to device, with no size limit, and can also make an encrypted link. See OmniBeam vs WeTransfer.

How is it different from Snapdrop, PairDrop or LocalSend?

All of them send files on a local network. OmniBeam adds pairing across networks, encrypted links, resume after a dropped connection, chat and apps for Windows and browsers. The comparisons go point by point.
