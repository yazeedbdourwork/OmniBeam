How to Use OmniBeam

🚀Get started6
  • Same Wi‑Fi: Open omnibeam.site on both devices and they appear automatically.
  • Send Files: Tap a device, or drag & drop files onto it. On a computer, hover over a device to choose Send files or Send message.
  • Send Text: Right-click a device to send text or links. On a phone, long-press it and choose Message.
  • Different Network: Tap the Link button at the top and create a 6‑digit code, then enter it or scan the QR code on the other device. After that, the two devices find each other automatically.
  • Pair with the camera: Tap the Link button at the top and scan its QR code with the other device's camera. It pairs straight away, with no code to type.
  • Name and picture: Tap your device name at the bottom to change it and pick an emoji picture that other devices see.
📤Send files and messages25
  • More…: Choose More… on a device (or long-press it on a phone) to take a photo or document scan, record a voice note, share your screen or send a secret note.
  • Chat: Choose Chat in a device's More… window to see everything you sent to it and received from it, messages and files, in one thread, and to send from there. A small number on a card means something new arrived.
  • Pinned note: In a chat, tap the pin button to keep a note at the top, like a Wi‑Fi password or an address. It stays on this device until you unpin it.
  • Open links: A link sent from another device has an Open link button. In Settings, Transfers, you can let links from your paired devices open by themselves.
  • Send a link that opens there: Tap the device, then More… and Send a link. Paste a web page or YouTube link and tap Send link. On the computer, tap Always open on the link's banner once: links from that device then open by themselves. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for omnibeam.site.
  • Folders: Drag a folder onto a device, or hover over it and choose Send folder. The other device saves it as a ZIP with the same folders inside.
  • Cancel or queue: Tap the ✕ on a device to stop a transfer. Sending more while a transfer runs puts it in line. With several devices visible, Send to everyone sends to all of them at once.
  • Send to all my devices: With two or more of your devices on screen, tap Send to all my devices to send files or a message to all of them at once. Paired devices that are offline get it as soon as they open OmniBeam.
  • Original or smaller photos: Photos go in their original size. For a slow connection, choose Smaller in More… (or in Settings): big photos are scaled to 2048 px and you see how much was saved. The originals are not changed.
  • Smaller videos: In Settings, Transfers, Photo size when sending can also make videos smaller, at 720p or 480p. In Chrome, Edge and Safari a one-minute clip takes a few seconds.
  • Scan to PDF: Choose Scan to PDF in More…, photograph one or more pages, and OmniBeam straightens them and sends one PDF. Colour or black and white.
  • Secret note: Send a password or code that is shown only once and never saved. By link: in Share files by link, choose Secret note instead, and the link opens only once.
  • Share your screen: On a computer, choose More… on a device, then Share my screen. The other device, even a tablet or a TV browser, sees it live. Nothing is recorded.
  • Interrupted transfer: If a page is reloaded or the connection drops, send the same files again within a day. Only the missing part is sent.
  • Continue on another device: If a send to a paired device stops halfway, tap the card that appears to send the rest to another of your paired devices.
  • Offline devices: Tap a greyed-out paired device to choose files. Keep the page open: they go as soon as that device opens OmniBeam.
  • Reply, react and more in a chat: The chat looks like a messenger: ✓ means sent, ✓✓ delivered and blue ✓✓ read, and you see when the other device is typing. Long-press or right-click a message, or tap its small arrow, to Reply, add a reaction, copy it, pin it or Delete for me. On a phone you can also swipe a message to reply. The round button sends; while the box is empty it is the paper clip for files.
  • Search a chat, and its media, files and links: In a chat, tap the search button at the top to find words in its messages. In the ⋮ menu, Media, files and links shows every photo, file and link of that chat in three tabs.
  • Forward or translate a message: In the menu of a message, Forward sends the message or file to another of your devices. Translate puts it into your language right on your device (recent Chrome and Edge on computers), and Show original goes back.
  • Send a message later: Write a message or choose files, then long-press or right-click the Send button and choose Send later (it is also in the ⋮ menu). Pick in 1 hour, tonight, tomorrow or a date and time, and tap Schedule. Keep OmniBeam open on this device: it sends at that time. You can Send now or cancel it before then.
  • Disappearing messages: In the ⋮ menu of a chat, choose Disappearing messages and pick 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days (or Off). Messages and files in that chat are then deleted after that time. The other device is told and sets the same time.
  • Voice messages in a chat: Hold the microphone button of the message box and speak, then let go to send; slide to the side to cancel. Or tap it once to record hands-free, then tap Send. Voice messages play right in the chat at 1×, 1.5× or 2× speed, and go straight to the other device, encrypted.
  • Edit a photo before sending: When you choose one photo in a chat, a small editor opens first: rotate it, crop it, draw on it with your finger or the mouse, or blur a face, a name or a number. Undo takes back the last change. Everything happens on your device; tap Send when it looks right.
  • Clear a chat, also on the other device: In the ⋮ menu of a chat, choose Clear chat. The messages and file entries with that device are removed from this device, with a few seconds to undo. Tick the box to also delete it on the other device: it is asked first and can choose Keep it or Delete here.
  • File tools before sending: In the ⋯ menu, or in More… on a device, tap File tools. Merge PDFs puts two or more PDFs together in the order you choose. Resize pictures makes photos smaller (for example 1920 px) as JPEG or WebP. Then send the result to a device or save it. Everything happens on your device.
🔗Share with other people16
  • Share by link: In the ⋯ menu at the top, tap Share files by link to upload files and get a link and QR code anyone can download from, until it expires. Files are encrypted on your device first.
  • Link options: A link can work for up to 7 days. You can also limit the number of downloads and add a PIN. Your links shows how many downloads are left.
  • Send by email or text: In the ⋯ menu, tap Send to email or phone (Send by email on a computer). Your email or messages app opens with the download link ready.
  • Request files: In the ⋯ menu at the top, tap Request files to get a link you can send to anyone. They open it on any device, with no app or pairing, and the files they pick arrive here, encrypted end to end.
  • On your website: In Request files, choose 1 year and tap Put this on your website to add a box to your website or blog where visitors can send you files, end-to-end encrypted. On WordPress, use the free OmniBeam plugin.
  • Public room: In the same window, create a 5‑letter room and share its code or link. Any number of devices can join, from any network.
  • Room password: Type a password before you create a public room, and only devices that know it can join. The password is not in the room link.
  • Team rooms: In the Link window, create a team room and share its fixed link. Every device that opens it once stays in the room, on any network.
  • Event photo album: In the ⋯ menu, tap Event photo album to make one QR code for a wedding, a party or a class. Guests scan it, add their photos and see everyone's, with no app, and you save them all at the end.
  • Album for the host: Open the album to stop or allow new photos and to delete any photo. Guests see right away when photos are stopped, and a deleted photo leaves every open album page within seconds.
  • Invite a friend: In the ⋯ menu, tap Invite a friend to make a picture with a QR code that opens OmniBeam, square or story size, and share it in a chat or a story.
  • Telegram: In Settings, tap Link Telegram. Files and text you send to the OmniBeam bot arrive here, and Send to Telegram in the ⋯ menu sends files to that chat (up to 20 MB to the bot, 50 MB from here, not end-to-end encrypted).
  • Share a whole folder by link: In Share files by link, tap 📁 Share a whole folder and choose a folder (up to 1,000 files). The person who opens the link downloads it as one ZIP with the same folders inside.
  • What people see when they open your link: The link opens a page with each file's name, size and a preview of pictures, videos, audio, PDFs and text. They can download one file or all of them as one ZIP. Big files come fast, in several parts at once, with the speed and time left, and carry on where they stopped if the connection drops. Send the link straight to WhatsApp or Telegram with the buttons under it, and tick Show the file name and a small picture when the link is pasted in a chat (not encrypted) if you want a preview in chats. Your links shows how many times each link was viewed and downloaded.
  • Know when someone downloads your link: In Share files by link, turn on Notify me when someone downloads my links. You then get a notification each time someone downloads one of your links (notifications must be on).
  • Group chat in a room: When three or more devices are in the same public room or team room, a Group chat button appears above the devices. Write there to everyone in the room at once, with each name in its own colour; voice messages work too. Only the devices in the room right now get it.
🗂️Received files and history7
  • History: Tap the download button at the top. Received shows files and messages from the last 7 days, Gallery shows the photos and videos with Save all, and Sent shows what you sent.
  • Send again: In History, open Sent and tap Send again to resend the same files. Export downloads the list as a spreadsheet (CSV).
  • Search the history: Type in the search box at the top of History to find a file, a device or words from a message on the tab that is open.
  • Send on or send again: In History, tap Send to… on a received file to send it to another device. In Sent, Send again still works after the page reloads: files up to 50 MB a send are kept on this device for as long as Keep history says.
  • Preview: In Received files and History, tap Preview to look at a PDF, picture, audio, text or code file before saving it.
  • Save to Photos: On iPhone and iPad, tap Save to Photos on a received photo or video, then Save Image or Save Video.
  • Save photos to the gallery as an album: When two or more photos or videos arrive on a phone, tap Save to gallery as an album in the window that shows them. On Android they go into one folder; on iPhone and iPad they are saved to Photos 20 at a time, and you can select them there and tap Add to Album.
💻Phone and computer together17
  • Share a folder: On a computer, tap Share a folder in the ⋯ menu, pick a folder and tick your paired phone. On the phone, tap the computer, then More… and Browse files to take any file from it. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Photo backup: On a computer, tap Photo backup in the ⋯ menu and choose a folder. On your paired phone, tap the computer, then More… and Back up photos, and choose your photos. Only the ones the computer doesn't have yet are sent, at full quality.
  • Sync a folder: On two computers with Chrome or Edge, tap Sync a folder in the ⋯ menu, choose a folder and the other computer. New and changed files copy over by themselves while OmniBeam is open on both.
  • Auto-send a folder: On a computer with Chrome or Edge, choose a folder in Settings, Clipboard. New files you put in it go to your phone by themselves while OmniBeam is open.
  • Clipboard sync: Pair your devices, then turn on Clipboard sync in Settings on both. Text you copy on one lands on the other's clipboard.
  • Clipboard history: With Clipboard sync on, OmniBeam can keep the last 20 texts in History, so you can copy one again later.
  • Phone as a webcam or mic: On a phone, choose More… on a computer, then Be its webcam or mic. The computer sees and hears it live, and Pop out makes a window that OBS can capture for Zoom, Meet or Teams.
  • TV mode: Open omnibeam.site/tv in your TV’s browser and scan the QR code with your phone. Photos and videos you send to the TV play as a full-screen slideshow.
  • Ring a device: Can't find your phone? Choose Ring this device in More… or in its chat. It rings for 5 seconds, and you can tap Ring again as often as you need.
  • Voice and video calls: In a chat, tap the phone button for a Voice call or the camera button for a Video call. The other device rings and taps Answer. During the call you can mute, turn the camera on or off, switch cameras and tap End call. Calls go straight between the two devices, encrypted, and nothing is recorded.
  • Present slides with your phone as the remote: On a computer, tap Present slides in the ⋯ menu and choose a PDF or photos (PowerPoint and Keynote can save a PDF). On your phone, tap the computer, then More… and Remote control: the phone moves to the next or previous slide and plays or pauses videos in OmniBeam, nothing else. A paired phone is allowed by itself; any other device has to be allowed on the computer first.
  • Screen share controls: While you share your screen, the bar at the top switches between Sharp text for documents and slides and Smooth motion for videos and games, pauses, shows a Black screen or stops. The person watching can zoom in, save a picture of the screen or keep it in a small Picture in picture window.
  • Notes and to-dos on all your devices: Tap Notes and to-dos in the ⋯ menu and write a note or a to-do list: add tasks, tick them off and Remove done tasks. Your paired devices get the same notes and lists whenever OmniBeam is open on them.
  • Favorite devices: Tap a paired device, then More… and Add to favorites, or tap ☆ next to it in the list of paired devices. Favorites come first, stay on the screen while offline, and connect by themselves whenever OmniBeam is open on both, so files start right away. The star on the card turns green once it is connected.
  • Copied pictures and screenshots: With clipboard sync on, a picture you copy, like a screenshot, goes to your paired devices too, not only text. A very big picture is better sent as a file.
  • Shared whiteboard: In More… on a device, tap Whiteboard. The other device is asked to open it, then you both draw on the same board at once, with 6 colours, 3 sizes and an eraser. Undo takes back your last line, Clear empties it for both, and Save picture keeps a PNG.
  • Back up new photos by themselves: On the phone, tap your computer, then More… and Back up photos, and turn on Back up new photos by themselves. On Android, pick the camera folder once (DCIM, then Camera): new photos go to the computer's backup folder whenever OmniBeam is open on both. On iPhone a one-tap card offers the backup when the computer is online.
⚙️Settings and privacy13
  • Auto-accept: In the Link window, turn on Auto-accept for a remembered device to receive its files without being asked.
  • Trusted devices: In the Link window, tick Trusted next to your own remembered devices. They always show on the main screen (greyed out while offline) and their files arrive without asking.
  • App lock: In Settings, Privacy, turn on App lock and choose a PIN, and fingerprint or face if your device has it. OmniBeam then asks for it before it opens. Files still arrive while it's locked.
  • Keep history: In Settings, Privacy, choose how long received files, messages and the Sent list stay on this device: 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days or 30 days.
  • Move to a new phone: In Settings, Device, tap Save backup file. It holds your paired devices, settings, pinned notes and chats, locked with a PIN or password. On the new phone, tap Open backup file.
  • Notifications: Turn on notifications in Settings. A paired device that wants to send you files while OmniBeam is closed then wakes this one. On iPhone, add OmniBeam to the Home Screen first.
  • Notification buttons: A message notification has Reply, a file notification has Save, and a ringing device shows Stop. Where the browser can't type in the notification, Reply opens the chat ready to type.
  • Text size and contrast: In Settings, Device, make the text Large or Largest, and choose High contrast for solid colours with clear edges.
  • Style: In Settings, Device, choose Classic or Liquid Glass, a see-through glass look, in light and dark.
  • Theme: In Settings, choose Automatic, Light or Dark.
  • Language: Choose your language at the bottom of the page: English, العربية, Français, Español, Türkçe, Deutsch, Português, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, Русский, 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어, Italiano or اردو.
  • Supporter: If you donated, type your supporter code in Settings for a ♥ on your card, four colour themes and extra pictures.
  • Check the safety code: In More… on a device, tap Safety code. Both devices show the same 12 digits when you are connected straight to each other; tap Show it on the other device too to compare. If the codes are different, don't send anything and connect again.
🧩Apps and add-ons7
  • Install: Tap Install at the bottom (Android, Chrome, Edge), or on iPhone tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: In Chrome, choose “Add to Home screen”. OmniBeam then appears in the Share menu of your other apps.
  • Windows: Get the OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store. It opens in its own window and appears in the Windows Share menu. Get the Windows app
  • Mac and Linux: Add OmniBeam to the right-click menu of Finder or your file manager: download and steps
  • Chrome extension: Send the page you're on, a link, an image or selected text from Chrome with one click. Get it from the Chrome Web Store. Add to Chrome
  • Firefox add-on: The same one-click extension is also made for Firefox. Add to Firefox
  • Command line: Developers can send, receive and share by link from a terminal with Node.js: npx omnibeam send file.zip Get it on npm
🛟Troubleshooting5
  • Connection check: Tap the network ID next to your device name to see why devices don't connect directly, with tips to fix it.
  • Speed test: Tap the network ID next to your device name, then Test speed next to a device, to see how fast the two can really transfer.
  • iPhone tip: Tap ⚡ Faster Wi‑Fi transfers at the bottom and allow the microphone once. Nothing is recorded; it only lets Safari connect directly over your Wi‑Fi. Choose “Allow” in Safari’s site settings and it turns on by itself next time.
  • Keep the screen on: Phones pause transfers when the screen turns off. Keep OmniBeam open until it finishes. If the screen turns off, open OmniBeam again and it continues by itself.
  • A device says Not answering: A device card and its chat show Online now when the other device answers, and Not answering when it is asleep or OmniBeam is in the background there. What you send waits and goes as soon as it is back on screen, so keep this page open.

Privacy Policy

OmniBeam sends your files and messages directly between your devices whenever it can, encrypted end to end by WebRTC. When a direct connection is not possible (for example behind a strict firewall or a VPN), they pass through our server instead, still encrypted end to end so the server can't read them, and are forwarded to the other device without being saved.

The details used to connect your devices are kept in memory only while you are online. OmniBeam uses no accounts or ads. If you allow it, Google Analytics counts visits to the site; your files and messages are never included. Your device name, paired devices and received-file history stay in your own browser.

When something goes wrong, for example a transfer fails, the page sends us a short problem report so we can fix it: what failed, the connection route and the browser type. It never includes file names, file contents, messages or addresses.



Scan to PDF makes the PDF on your device, and the chat view only shows the history already on it. If you use a supporter code, the server checks it when you connect and keeps a list of the codes it made.

Taking a photo, recording a voice note and sharing your screen use your camera, microphone or screen only after you allow it, and only while that window is open. They go straight to the device you chose, encrypted end to end, and are never saved on our server. Secret notes are not kept on the device that receives them, and a secret-note link is deleted from the server as soon as it is read.

About OmniBeam

OmniBeam is a seamless, zero-install WebRTC peer-to-peer transfer tool designed to make sharing files and clipboard notes across local and remote devices instant and private.

OmniBeam is free, with no ads. People who donate with the ♥ button can get a supporter code as a thank-you: typed in Settings, it adds a ♥ to their device's card, four colour themes and extra pictures.
