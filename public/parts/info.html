How to Use OmniBeam

  1. Same Wi‑Fi: Open omnibeam.site on both devices and they appear automatically.
  2. Send Files: Tap a device, or drag & drop files onto it. On a computer, hover over a device to choose Send files or Send message.
  3. Send Text: Right-click a device to send text or links. On a phone, long-press it and choose Message.
  4. More…: Choose More… on a device (or long-press it on a phone) to take a photo or document scan, record a voice note, share your screen or send a secret note.
  5. Share your screen: On a computer, choose More… on a device, then Share my screen. The other device, even a tablet or a TV browser, sees it live. Nothing is recorded.
  6. Secret note: Send a password or code that is shown only once and never saved. By link: in Share files by link, choose Secret note instead, and the link opens only once.
  7. Preview: In Received files and History, tap Preview to look at a PDF, picture, audio, text or code file before saving it.
  8. Different Network: Tap the Link button at the top and create a 6‑digit code, then enter it or scan the QR code on the other device. After that, the two devices find each other automatically.
  9. Public room: In the same window, create a 5‑letter room and share its code or link. Any number of devices can join, from any network.
  10. Folders: Drag a folder onto a device, or hover over it and choose Send folder. The other device saves it as a ZIP with the same folders inside.
  11. Cancel or queue: Tap the ✕ on a device to stop a transfer. Sending more while a transfer runs puts it in line. With several devices visible, Send to everyone sends to all of them at once.
  12. Auto-accept: In the Link window, turn on Auto-accept for a remembered device to receive its files without being asked.
  13. Trusted devices: In the Link window, tick Trusted next to your own remembered devices. They always show on the main screen (greyed out while offline) and their files arrive without asking.
  14. Original or smaller photos: Photos go in their original size. For a slow connection, choose Smaller in More… (or in Settings): big photos are scaled to 2048 px and you see how much was saved. The originals are not changed.
  15. Chat: Choose Chat in a device's More… window to see everything you sent to it and received from it, messages and files, in one thread, and to send from there. A small number on a card means something new arrived.
  16. Scan to PDF: Choose Scan to PDF in More…, photograph one or more pages, and OmniBeam straightens them and sends one PDF. Colour or black and white.
  17. Supporter: If you donated, type your supporter code in Settings for a ♥ on your card, four colour themes and extra pictures.
  18. Room password: Type a password before you create a public room, and only devices that know it can join. The password is not in the room link.
  19. Interrupted transfer: If a page is reloaded or the connection drops, send the same files again within a day. Only the missing part is sent.
  20. Share by link: In the ⋯ menu at the top, tap Share files by link to upload files and get a link and QR code anyone can download from, until it expires. Files are encrypted on your device first.
  21. Request files: In the ⋯ menu at the top, tap Request files to get a link you can send to anyone. They open it on any device, with no app or pairing, and the files they pick arrive here, encrypted end to end.
  22. On your website: In Request files, choose 1 year and tap Put this on your website to add a box to your website or blog where visitors can send you files, end-to-end encrypted. On WordPress, use the free OmniBeam plugin.
  23. Telegram: In Settings, tap Link Telegram. Files and text you send to the OmniBeam bot arrive here, and Send to Telegram in the ⋯ menu sends files to that chat (up to 20 MB to the bot, 50 MB from here, not end-to-end encrypted).
  24. TV mode: Open omnibeam.site/tv in your TV’s browser and scan the QR code with your phone. Photos and videos you send to the TV play as a full-screen slideshow.
  25. Connection check: Tap the network ID next to your device name to see why devices don't connect directly, with tips to fix it.
  26. Install: Tap Install at the bottom (Android, Chrome, Edge), or on iPhone tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.
  27. History: Tap the download button at the top. Received shows files and messages from the last 7 days, Gallery shows the photos and videos with Save all, and Sent shows what you sent.
  28. Send again: In History, open Sent and tap Send again to resend the same files. Export downloads the list as a spreadsheet (CSV).
  29. Android: In Chrome, choose “Add to Home screen”. OmniBeam then appears in the Share menu of your other apps.
  30. Windows: Get the OmniBeam app from the Microsoft Store. It opens in its own window and appears in the Windows Share menu. Get the Windows app
  31. Mac and Linux: Add OmniBeam to the right-click menu of Finder or your file manager: download and steps
  32. Clipboard sync: Pair your devices, then turn on Clipboard sync in Settings on both. Text you copy on one lands on the other's clipboard.
  33. Speed test: Tap the network ID next to your device name, then Test speed next to a device, to see how fast the two can really transfer.
  34. Pair with the camera: Tap the Link button at the top and scan its QR code with the other device's camera. It pairs straight away, with no code to type.
  35. Notifications: Turn on notifications in Settings. A paired device that wants to send you files while OmniBeam is closed then wakes this one. On iPhone, add OmniBeam to the Home Screen first.
  36. Offline devices: Tap a greyed-out paired device to choose files. Keep the page open: they go as soon as that device opens OmniBeam.
  37. Chrome extension: Send the page you're on, a link, an image or selected text from Chrome with one click. Get it from the Chrome Web Store. Add to Chrome
  38. Send by email or text: In the ⋯ menu, tap Send to email or phone (Send by email on a computer). Your email or messages app opens with the download link ready.
  39. Link options: A link can work for up to 7 days. You can also limit the number of downloads and add a PIN. Your links shows how many downloads are left.
  40. Continue on another device: If a send to a paired device stops halfway, tap the card that appears to send the rest to another of your paired devices.
  41. Team rooms: In the Link window, create a team room and share its fixed link. Every device that opens it once stays in the room, on any network.
  42. Name and picture: Tap your device name at the bottom to change it and pick an emoji picture that other devices see.
  43. Theme: In Settings, choose Automatic, Light or Dark.
  44. Save to Photos: On iPhone and iPad, tap Save to Photos on a received photo or video, then Save Image or Save Video.
  45. Keep the screen on: Phones pause transfers when the screen turns off. Keep OmniBeam open until it finishes. If the screen turns off, open OmniBeam again and it continues by itself.
  46. Event photo album: In the ⋯ menu, tap Event photo album to make one QR code for a wedding, a party or a class. Guests scan it, add their photos and see everyone's, with no app, and you save them all at the end.
  47. Album for the host: Open the album to stop or allow new photos and to delete any photo. Guests see right away when photos are stopped, and a deleted photo leaves every open album page within seconds.
  48. Share a folder: On a computer, tap Share a folder in the ⋯ menu, pick a folder and tick your paired phone. On the phone, tap the computer, then More… and Browse files to take any file from it. Nothing is uploaded.
  49. Sync a folder: On two computers with Chrome or Edge, tap Sync a folder in the ⋯ menu, choose a folder and the other computer. New and changed files copy over by themselves while OmniBeam is open on both.
  50. Send to all my devices: With two or more of your devices on screen, tap Send to all my devices to send files or a message to all of them at once. Paired devices that are offline get it as soon as they open OmniBeam.
  51. Ring a device: Can't find your phone? Choose Ring this device in More… or at the top of the chat. It rings loudly until someone taps Stop.
  52. Open links: A link sent from another device has an Open link button. In Settings, Transfers, you can let links from your paired devices open by themselves.
  53. Pinned note: In a chat, tap the pin button to keep a note at the top, like a Wi‑Fi password or an address. It stays on this device until you unpin it.
  54. Invite a friend: In the ⋯ menu, tap Invite a friend to make a picture with a QR code that opens OmniBeam, square or story size, and share it in a chat or a story.
  55. App lock: In Settings, Privacy, turn on App lock and choose a PIN, and fingerprint or face if your device has it. OmniBeam then asks for it before it opens. Files still arrive while it's locked.
  56. Keep history: In Settings, Privacy, choose how long received files, messages and the Sent list stay on this device: 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days or 30 days.
  57. Smaller videos: In Settings, Transfers, Photo size when sending can also make videos smaller, at 720p or 480p. In Chrome, Edge and Safari a one-minute clip takes a few seconds.
  58. Move to a new phone: In Settings, Device, tap Save backup file. It holds your paired devices, settings, pinned notes and chats, locked with a PIN or password. On the new phone, tap Open backup file.
  59. Notification buttons: A message notification has Reply, a file notification has Save, and a ringing device shows Stop. Where the browser can't type in the notification, Reply opens the chat ready to type.
  60. Text size and contrast: In Settings, Device, make the text Large or Largest, and choose High contrast for solid colours with clear edges.
  61. Style: In Settings, Device, choose Classic or Liquid Glass, a see-through glass look, in light and dark.
  62. Auto-send a folder: On a computer with Chrome or Edge, choose a folder in Settings, Clipboard. New files you put in it go to your phone by themselves while OmniBeam is open.
  63. Clipboard history: With Clipboard sync on, OmniBeam can keep the last 20 texts in History, so you can copy one again later.
  64. Firefox add-on: The same one-click extension is also made for Firefox. Add to Firefox
  65. Language: Choose your language at the bottom of the page: English, العربية, Français, Español, Türkçe, Deutsch, Português, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, Русский, 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어, Italiano or اردو.
  66. iPhone tip: Tap ⚡ Faster Wi‑Fi transfers at the bottom and allow the microphone once. Nothing is recorded; it only lets Safari connect directly over your Wi‑Fi. Choose “Allow” in Safari’s site settings and it turns on by itself next time.

Privacy Policy

OmniBeam sends your files and messages directly between your devices whenever it can, encrypted end to end by WebRTC. When a direct connection is not possible (for example behind a strict firewall or a VPN), they pass through our server instead, still encrypted end to end so the server can't read them, and are forwarded to the other device without being saved.

The details used to connect your devices are kept in memory only while you are online. OmniBeam uses no accounts or ads. If you allow it, Google Analytics counts visits to the site; your files and messages are never included. Your device name, paired devices and received-file history stay in your own browser.

When something goes wrong, for example a transfer fails, the page sends us a short problem report so we can fix it: what failed, the connection route and the browser type. It never includes file names, file contents, messages or addresses.



Scan to PDF makes the PDF on your device, and the chat view only shows the history already on it. If you use a supporter code, the server checks it when you connect and keeps a list of the codes it made.

Taking a photo, recording a voice note and sharing your screen use your camera, microphone or screen only after you allow it, and only while that window is open. They go straight to the device you chose, encrypted end to end, and are never saved on our server. Secret notes are not kept on the device that receives them, and a secret-note link is deleted from the server as soon as it is read.

About OmniBeam

OmniBeam is a seamless, zero-install WebRTC peer-to-peer transfer tool designed to make sharing files and clipboard notes across local and remote devices instant and private.

OmniBeam is free, with no ads. People who donate with the ♥ button can get a supporter code as a thank-you: typed in Settings, it adds a ♥ to their device's card, four colour themes and extra pictures.
