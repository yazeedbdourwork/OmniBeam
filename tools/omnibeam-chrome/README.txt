OmniBeam for Chrome and Edge
============================

Send the page you're on, a link, an image or selected text to your phone or another computer, with one click.

Install (once)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere it can stay, e.g. Documents\omnibeam-chrome. (Chrome loads it from there:
   don't delete or move the folder afterwards.)
2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions  (in Edge: edge://extensions)
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right in Chrome, on the left in Edge).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the omnibeam-chrome folder (the one with manifest.json in it).
5. Optional: click the puzzle-piece button next to the address bar and pin OmniBeam, so its button is always there.

Use
- Keep an OmniBeam tab open (https://omnibeam.site). Your other devices show up there as usual.
- Right-click anywhere on a page, on a link, on an image or on selected text: Send to OmniBeam > the device.
  It goes straight there; the other device taps Accept (or not, if you turned on Auto-accept for this computer).
- Or click the OmniBeam button in the toolbar and pick a device: it sends this page, or the selected text.
- "Choose a device…" (or no OmniBeam tab open yet) switches to the OmniBeam tab: tap the device there.

Images
- Images from most websites are sent as image files. Some websites don't let extensions read their images; then
  the image's address is sent instead. To send images from every website, open the toolbar button > More and
  tick "Send images from any website" (Chrome asks you to allow it).

Update
- Unzip the new version over the old folder, then click the reload arrow on OmniBeam in chrome://extensions.

Privacy
- The extension only talks to the OmniBeam tab in your browser. What you send goes from that tab to your device
  the same way as files you send from the page. It has no access to other websites, except reading an image
  you right-click (and every image only if you tick the option above).
